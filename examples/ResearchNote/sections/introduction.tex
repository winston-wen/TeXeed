\section{Introduction}

Good typesetting makes mathematical ideas easier to follow. The principles of
the TeX system are described by Knuth~\cite{knuth1984texbook}; LaTeX provides a
document-oriented approach to the same foundation~\cite{lamport1994latex}.

\subsection{A familiar equation}
The relation between energy and mass is written as
\begin{equation}
  E = mc^2.
  \label{eq:energy}
\end{equation}
Equation~\eqref{eq:energy} is used again in Section~\ref{sec:method}.
